What to bring

Bring your forms and your ID, the rest is easy, leave it to me!

Everyone

  • Photo ID for you (and spouse)
  • Social Security cards or ITIN letters for everyone on the return
  • W-2s from every job
  • 1099s (interest, unemployment, retirement, gig apps)
  • 1095-A if you had Marketplace health insurance
  • Last year's tax return
  • Bank routing & account number for direct deposit

Families & homeowners

  • Childcare provider name, address, tax ID and amount paid
  • 1098-T tuition forms and student loan interest (1098-E)
  • Mortgage interest (1098) and property tax statements
  • Charitable donation receipts

Self-employed & business

  • 1099-NEC / 1099-K and income records
  • Expense totals: supplies, phone, software, advertising
  • Mileage log or vehicle expenses
  • Home office size, if you use one
  • Estimated tax payments you made
  • Profit & loss statement or bank statements
