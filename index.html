What to bring to your appointment

Bring what applies to you. Not sure? Call or text and ask. We'll tell you exactly what you need.

Everyone

  • Photo ID (driver's license or state ID) for you and your spouse
  • Social Security cards or ITIN letters for everyone on the return
  • Birth dates for you, your spouse and dependents
  • Last year's tax return, if you have it
  • IRS Identity Protection PIN, if you were issued one
  • A voided check or bank letter for direct deposit

Income

  • W-2s from every job
  • 1099-NEC, 1099-K or 1099-MISC (gig, contract or side work)
  • 1099-G (unemployment) and SSA-1099 (Social Security)
  • 1099-R (retirement or pension)
  • 1099-INT / 1099-DIV (bank interest or dividends)

Credits & deductions

  • Childcare provider's name, address, tax ID and amount paid
  • 1098-T (college tuition) and 1098-E (student loan interest)
  • 1098 (mortgage interest) and property tax statements
  • 1095-A if you had Marketplace health insurance

Self-employed & business

  • Total income records (1099s, app statements, invoices)
  • Business expenses: supplies, phone, equipment, fees
  • Mileage log for business driving
  • Home office size, if you work from home

For your security, please don't text or email Social Security numbers or tax documents. Bring them to your appointment.
